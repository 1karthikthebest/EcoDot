# 🌱 EcoDot — Small Actions. Big Impact.

**An AI-powered concept to encourage everyday environmental actions and responsible waste management.**

## 📌 About the Project

EcoDot is a proposed application designed to motivate people to participate in small environmental activities. Users complete environmental missions, submit video evidence, and earn EcoPoints when their submissions are approved.

Our goal is to make environmental participation more engaging, measurable, and community-driven.

## 🚨 The Problem

Waste management is a major global challenge.

- Improperly managed waste can pollute land, water, and air.
- Littering and poor waste segregation affect communities.
- People need accessible ways to participate in local environmental activities.

According to the United Nations Environment Programme's *Global Waste Management Outlook 2024*, global municipal solid waste generation was approximately 2.1 billion tonnes in 2023 and could reach 3.8 billion tonnes by 2050.

## 💡 Our Idea

EcoDot turns everyday environmental activities into missions.

Users can:
- Discover environmental missions.
- Complete activities in their communities.
- Upload video evidence.
- Receive EcoPoints for approved missions.
- Track their progress and participation.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Choose a mission** — Select an available EcoMission.
2. **Complete the activity** — Carry out the environmental task safely.
3. **Submit evidence** — Upload a video showing the activity.
4. **AI assessment** — AI analyses visible evidence against the mission requirements.
5. **Verification** — Unclear submissions can be sent for human review.
6. **Earn EcoPoints** — Approved missions add points to the user's account.

## 🧠 AI Technology

The proposed AI system may use:

- **Object recognition:** Identify visible objects such as bottles, bags, and waste bins.
- **Mission assessment:** Compare submitted evidence with mission requirements.
- **Duplicate detection:** Flag potentially repeated submissions.
- **Human review:** Help resolve uncertain or suspicious submissions.

AI is intended to assist verification, not act as unquestionable proof that an activity happened.

## 🌍 Expected Impact

EcoDot aims to:

- Encourage responsible waste disposal.
- Promote waste segregation and recycling.
- Increase participation in community-cleaning activities.
- Build environmental awareness among students and communities.

These are intended outcomes; they have not yet been demonstrated through a real-world pilot.

## ⚠️ Challenges and Safety

| Challenge | Proposed approach |
|---|---|
| Fake or reused submissions | Duplicate detection and human review |
| Incorrect AI decisions | Manual review and resubmission |
| Privacy concerns | Minimise personal data collection |
| Unsafe activities | Use clearly defined, safe missions |

## 🚀 Future Plans

Possible future improvements include:

- School and community leaderboards.
- Environmental challenges.
- Partnerships with recycling organisations.
- More mission categories.
- A pilot programme in a school or neighbourhood.

## 📊 Measuring Success

A future pilot could measure:

- Completed and approved missions.
- Quantity of waste collected and properly handed over.
- Repeat participation.
- Number of participating schools or communities.
